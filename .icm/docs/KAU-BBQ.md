 Kau Barbecue officially opened its permanent location in Malveira, Portugal on July 28, 2026, following years of successful pop-ups and festival appearances.  The restaurant, created by Rui and Vera Matias, is situated at R. Dr. José Eduardo Esteves h1, 2665-248 Malveira and operates from Thursday to Sunday. 

The venue offers two distinct dining experiences: traditional table service and an authentic Texan counter service where trays are assembled, weighed, and served directly to customers.  The menu focuses on high-quality American-style barbecue meats sold by weight, including Brisket Black Angus (58€/kg), Beefrib Black Angus (70€/kg), and Tomahawk Wagyu SRF (160€/unit). 

Key operational details include:

Hours: Lunch (12:00–15:00) and Dinner (19:00–22:00). 
Reservations: Mandatory online booking; the restaurant reached full capacity within hours of opening. 
Price Range: Approximately €15–€20 per person for standard items, though premium cuts are sold by weight. 
Delivery: Available via Glovo starting in July 2026.
The Malveira location, described as the brand's "mother house," features a large smoker nicknamed "Godzilla" and aims to be one of the largest steakhouses in the country.
